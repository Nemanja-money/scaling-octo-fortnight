Good to know

Frequently asked.

Is this a real AI generator?

This working prototype creates a tailored draft from built-in templates in your browser. It does not yet call a live AI model. The pricing buttons select a plan and take customers into setup, but real checkout, paid access, client logins and cloud storage still require integrations.

Can I use this for paying clients?

You can use the site as a demo and use its draft ideas as a starting point, but review and customize every plan before delivery. Confirm business facts, offers, links, dates and permissions. Don't claim the tool uses live AI until you connect it.

Does it cost anything?

The files can be hosted on a free static hosting service, subject to that provider's terms and limits. A custom domain, live AI API, automated workflows and payment processing may cost money.

Can customers save their own calendars?

This demo has a copy-plan button but no user accounts or cloud storage. A production client portal would need a backend or a suitable third-party service.
